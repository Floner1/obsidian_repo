Pattern: [Independent clause A]; [conjunctive adverb], [independent clause B].

Words like however, though, therefore, thus, moreover, nevertheless are conjunctive adverbs. When one sits at the boundary between two independent clauses (both sides could stand alone as full sentences), you need:

- A semicolon right before the adverb, marking where one clause ends.
- A comma right after the adverb, separating it from the clause that follows.
