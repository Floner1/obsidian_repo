**GRAMMAR:**
- Independent clause: clause that make sense on it own
- Dependent clause: clause not make sense on own
- ![[DAY 30 29-07-2026 independent vs dependent clause.png]]
- IC NEED:
	- Subject, smth that does action
	- main verb
	- no contain WASABI verb: while wen since after as because before if 
- IC DC SEPERATIONS:
- IC IC:
	- full stop.
	- semi colon;
	- comma then fanboys,
- IC DC:
	- comma,
	- nothing
- dashes are the same as commas
- colons are used for lists or presenting more info about smth, eg: peter has a ball: the ball is red. 
- semi colon used to separate 2 ic 
- if items in list is long, then use ; as separation, complex lists:
- complex list: these are the places i travelled to: paris, france; hanoi, vietnam; vienna, austria; etc.
- parenthesizes and dashes do same thing, dashes more abrupt and parenthesizes less abrupt

- VERBS:
	- match subject with verb that goes with it
	- SINGULAR VERBS:
		- verbs describing 1 thing
		- u gotta put an s at the end of verb, opposite to nouns
		- she walks to the store
	- PLURAL VERBS:
		- verbs describing more than 1 thing
		- no s at end of verb, opposite to nouns
		- they walk to the store
