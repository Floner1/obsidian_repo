sale loads/charge:
fee used by mutual funds to compensate brokers who sell shares.
can be back or front end, chosen by u
sales charge discounts: may give discount on sales charge if large investment

front end sale load:
- might pay this fee when u purchase mf, 
- calculated based on % of sale price
- paid out of money u used to invest
- reduces amount of money have to invest
- eg:
	- $10k to buy mf with 5% frond end sales load. $500 deducted and given to selling broker, u only got 9.5k remaining
