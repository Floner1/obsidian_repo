# PPC Study Plan

## 2. Explain why it's concave
Factors of production aren't perfectly substitutable between uses. Fertile land grows wheat well but is poor for mining, and vice versa. As you push more resources into one good, you're forced to use increasingly unsuitable resources for it, so the opportunity cost of each extra unit rises. This is the law of increasing opportunity cost. Say this in your own words, not just the label.
